    How this is counted
    Procurement posting
    The title has a procurement term, or the agency filed the role under the Procurement field. Roles that only mention procurement in the duties are left out unless that option is turned on.
    First seen
    The first daily snapshot that contained the posting. History starts on , so roles already open that day count from then, not from when they were posted.
    New postings
    Postings first seen in a period. In the first month of history, only roles also posted that month are counted, so the backlog open on day one is not drawn as a spike.
    Open at end of period
    Postings first seen on or before the last day of the period and still listed on that day. For the current period, the last processed day is used.
    Quarters
    Calendar quarters. A quarter is compared only when every day of it is inside the history. The current quarter is shown as “to date” and never compared. Year-on-year comparison turns on once a full quarter has a full quarter a year earlier, from .
    Increase or decrease
    Called only when both periods have at least postings and differ by at least postings and %. Otherwise the text says “about the same” or “too few to compare”.
    Closing soon
    Open postings whose closing date is within days of the last processed day. Some postings have no closing date.
    Contract type
    Taken from the source employment type. Mapping is in config/dashboard.yml.
    Minimum experience
    The lowest years of experience stated on the posting, from the structured field or the requirement text.
    Sources and limits
    Public Careers@Gov postings republished by OpenGovSG. Postings that opened and closed between snapshots are missing. Links to closed postings can stop working. Matching is rule-based, not a model, and should be checked before it is used as a target.
    Public Careers@Gov postings as republished by OpenGovSG. No applicant or candidate information is held.
