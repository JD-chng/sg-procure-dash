Executive overview

Definitions

How a number on this page is produced
Core procurement role
The title has a procurement term, or the source field or functional area is Procurement. This is the default population for every chart, card, and the open-roles table.
Duties-only match
The title and field are not procurement, but the duties contain a phrase such as “manage procurement” or “procurement of”. These roles are hidden until “Include roles matched only from the duties” is checked. A lone “tender” and “vendor” is still not enough, and “contract employee” stays excluded.
Sector
Keyword score on the title and duties. Agency name never assigns a sector. A tie or a weak score becomes Other / Unclassified.
Career level
Parsed from explicit years. Fresh-graduate language maps to entry level. One to three years, inside a narrow range, is early career. Four to seven is mid-career. Eight or more, including “8+ years”, is experienced. Ranges that cross those bands, and open-ended minima below eight years, stay broad.
Observed span
Days from the first snapshot that contained the posting to the last. A posting that drops off and returns keeps the original first-seen date, and the span includes the gap. The median is shown only when at least ten postings have left the extract.
Snapshot coverage
History begins at the first usable upstream snapshot. The trend does not plot earlier months. Roles already listed in that first month are excluded from the trend unless their posting date is also in that month.
Competencies
Editable phrase list aligned to public procurement stages. A posting may carry more than one label.
